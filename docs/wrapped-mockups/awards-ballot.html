The five awards

Player of the SeasonA shortlist of 5, drawn from the season's numbers (apps, goals, MOTM wins, Player of the Month), then the squad votes. The 5 here are an example from today's numbers.
Goal of the SeasonAdmins nominate 3 or 4 goals during the season with a one-line description, because there's no video. The descriptions here are examples.
Best NewcomerAnyone whose first game was on or after 1 Sep (everyone else joined in the opening fortnight), with 3+ games. That's 8 players today.
Moment of the SeasonReal moments picked from the data: Callum's 7, Jacob's 6, Michael's 5, Lewis's back-to-back hat-tricks, the 8–2.
Team-mate of the SeasonOpen vote: anyone with 3+ games. "The one who makes Thursday night better." It gives the non-scorers a shot at a trophy.

Rules

  • Who votes: anyone who's played 3+ games this season. That's 33 players today.
  • You can't vote for yourself. You show greyed out as "(you)".
  • Any award can be skipped. You can change your votes until it closes.
  • Secret ballot: admins see the turnout ("19 of 33"), not the picks, so there are no spoilers for the organisers either.
  • Ties: the most MOTM wins over the season breaks a tie. Otherwise it's a shared award, two trophies.

Timeline

  1. Tue 1 Dec, 8am: the ballot opens. Push: "The 2026 Awards are open. Your vote counts."
  2. The gold banner sits on top of Fixtures until you've voted, then turns to "You've voted ✓".
  3. 3 days before it closes: one reminder push, only to people who haven't voted.
  4. The night before the last game: the ballot closes.
  5. The morning after the last game: Season One Wrapped opens, and Awards Night reveals each winner, with their trophy going into the cabinet.
