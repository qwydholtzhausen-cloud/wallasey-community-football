How the awards are decided

  • Player of the Season: a shortlist of 5 comes from the stats (Player of the Month winners and most MOTM votes), then the squad votes.
  • Goal of the Season: admins shortlist 4–6 goals with a one-line description each, then the squad votes.
  • Best Newcomer: anyone whose first game was this season, squad vote.
  • Moment of the Season: free-text nominations, admins pick the shortlist, the squad votes. The winner's quote goes on the card.
  • Game of the Season needs no vote. It's the highest average "How was tonight?" rating.

What makes it hit

  • Every card is about them, or about people they know.
  • Cards only show when they flatter: "Top 5%" picks the stat where the player ranks best.
  • The player type is the one people will screenshot, and it's rare on purpose.
  • The finale reveal is shared: everyone sees the same winner at the same moment.
