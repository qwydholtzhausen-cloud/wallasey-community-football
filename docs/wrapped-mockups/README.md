# Wrapped mock-ups (dummy data)

Design references saved 29 Sep 2026. Open the HTML files in a browser from this folder. Images load from `public/`.

- `season-one.html`: the Season One (2026) Wrapped. It has a black-and-gold identity and a launch section (countdown banner the night before, then an 8am push the morning after the last December game). Act One is "Your Season", 12 personal cards. Act Two is "Awards Night": Goal of the Season, Best Newcomer, Moment of the Season, Game of the Season (from ratings), the roll of honour, a drumroll, the Player of the Season reveal, the club's year, and Season Two with a Book button.
  - Live copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/4QfepmdpRUTpHDEwSsyDPN
- `wrapped-editions.html`: the October "FRIGHT LIGHTS" and November "BANGERS" monthly Wrappeds. They mix existing and new cards, use rotating photos and pick the best 8 cards per player. There is no December monthly, because Season One replaces it.
  - Live copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/L5aLqurW3gCu94HeoSesxw
- `awards-ballot.html` (saved 10 Oct 2026): how players vote in the Season One awards. A gold Fixtures banner opens the vote, followed by an intro, five award screens, a ballot-paper review and a sealed envelope dropping into a ballot box. It uses real players and moments as of 8 Oct; the Goal of the Season descriptions and turnout are examples. The award list is to be confirmed by the user later. It is self-contained (images embedded).
  - Live copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/PsHSembsPNjwDALgZRwpgi
- `team-of-the-month.html` (saved 10 Oct 2026): Team of the Month V2 with real September 2026 data. Floodlights come on, gold-edged player cards flip onto a perspective pitch (back three, midfield, front two), and the Player of the Month (Purya) arrives last in gold as captain. Then a banner and the points table. Open calls: 8 or XI, the points formula, a trophy cabinet badge. Not built yet.
  - Live copy: https://claude.ai/artifact/3QdpiLm6ui5Sx7pD1HFJdu
