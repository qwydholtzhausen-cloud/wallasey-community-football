SEASON ONE

No December monthly. December goes into the Season One Wrapped instead: 15–20 cards, its own black-and-gold look, awards night, the club's year, every Player of the Month. It gets its own mock-up and build, starting early November.

A name that fits the player

The theme word stays the same for everyone. The line under it changes: a scorer's Fright Lights says "Scary in front of goal", a defender's says "The brick wall of Halloween". Bangers is "Your November, lit up" or "Quiet on the pitch, loud in the group".
