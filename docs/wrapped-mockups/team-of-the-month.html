The reveal (about 5 seconds)

  • The pitch is dark. The two floodlight pylons switch on and the beams sweep down.
  • The back three drop onto the pitch as cards, flipping in one at a time with a gold shine, then the midfield three, then the front two.
  • Last, a spotlight comes on and the Player of the Month card arrives in solid gold, wearing the captain's C.
  • A gold banner unrolls, and the points table fills in underneath.

Each card shows

Their photo, first name, points, and their month in one line: goals, wins and Man of the Match wins.

The shape

We don't record positions, so it's shaped by goals: the top two scorers up front, the next three in midfield, the rest at the back. The captain is always the Player of the Month, wherever they land.
